# Weekly digest config

Fill every field. Keep the filled file private; it names your channels, work and goals. The digest skill asks once for anything missing and never guesses.

- output_path: <folder for the digest, for example ~/notes/digest/YYYY-Www.md>
- window: <for example "last 7 days ending at run time">
- timezone: <for example UTC>
- repositories: <forge repositories to watch, for example owner/product, owner/shared-ui>
- area_labels: <labels or groups that mark your area, for example team::platform>
- product_areas: <features to watch for incidents, for example search, billing>
- release_sources: <release or changelog feeds to check, with URLs>
- announcement_channels: <chat channels for product and launch announcements>
- area_channels: <chat channels for your discipline's announcements, deprecations, lint rules>
- incident_channels: <chat channels where incidents are announced>
- ai_channels: <chat channels for AI news, with IDs if the tool needs them>
- opening_channels: <chat channels to scan for calls for help or volunteers>
- owned_work: <files or links describing work you already own>
- goals: <file or short list of your growth goals, used to rank openings>
- time_budget: <file or note with your available time this week>
- user_handle: <forge username, to check your own merged work>
